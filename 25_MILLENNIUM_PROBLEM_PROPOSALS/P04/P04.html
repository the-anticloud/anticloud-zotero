### 1. Core Hypothesis

We hypothesize that the continuum limit ($a \to 0$) of the Wilson lattice action for a compact simple Lie group $G$ (e.g., $SU(3)$) defines a unique, reflection-positive measure $\mu_{YM}$ on the space of tempered distributions $\mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^4)$ modulo gauge transformations. Crucially, we posit that the "mass gap" is not an artifact of perturbation theory but a geometric consequence of the **topological charge density fluctuations** stabilizing the vacuum against infrared divergences, effectively rendering the gluon field massive despite the absence of a Higgs mechanism.

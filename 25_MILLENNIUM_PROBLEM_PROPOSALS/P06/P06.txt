### 2. Mathematical Framework

This allows us to replace the study of $L(E, s)$ with $L(f_E, s)$, enabling the use of automorphic methods and complex analysis.

*   **Euler Systems:** Construct families of cohomology classes $(c_K)_{K}$ in Galois cohomology groups $H^1(G_K, E[p^\infty])$ indexed by abelian extensions $K/\mathbb{Q}$, satisfying specific compatibility conditions under corestriction.
*   **Kolyvagin's Method:** Use these classes to construct "Heegner points" or generalized Stark-Heegner elements that generate the Mordell-Weil group and control the size of the Selmer group.

#### C. The Bloch-Kato Formalism

*   **Key Tool:** The explicit reciprocity law connecting $p$-adic $L$-functions (constructed by Hida, Perrin-Riou, etc.) to the structure of the Selmer group over cyclotomic towers.

#### D. $p$-adic Interpolation

* **Key Distinction:** Unlike AdS/CFT which relies on a specific large-$N$ limit and negative cosmological constant, this hypothesis asserts that *any* 4D QFT satisfying asymptotic safety conditions will generate a dynamical Lorentzian geometry in the IR, unifying gravity with the Standard Model sectors without requiring extra dimensions or supersymmetry.

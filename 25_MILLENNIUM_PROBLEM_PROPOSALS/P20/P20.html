#### 1) Core Hypothesis
The resolution to the Black Hole Information Paradox lies in the **Generalized Entropy Principle** applied to **Entanglement Islands**.

Contrary to the semi-classical expectation that information is lost as the black hole evaporates (leading to mixed states), we hypothesize that the von Neumann entropy of the Hawking radiation, $S_{\text{rad}}$, does not grow indefinitely. Instead, at the "Page time," the quantum extremal surface (QES) defining the entanglement wedge of the radiation undergoes a topological phase transition. This transition incorporates a region inside the black hole horizon—the **Island**—into the entanglement wedge of the radiation. Consequently, the generalized entropy of the radiation decreases after the Page time, recovering the unitary Page curve and preserving information without violating local effective field theory outside the horizon.

Information is not stored on the stretched horizon but is non-locally encoded via the bulk-boundary correspondence, where the interior geometry emerges from the entanglement structure of the boundary state.

#### 2) Mathematical Framework

**A. Generalized Entropy ($S_{\text{gen}}$):**
For a region $R$ (the Hawking radiation collected far away), the entropy is defined by minimizing over all possible codimension-2 surfaces $\gamma$ homologous to $R$:
$$ S(R) = \min_{\mathcal{I}} \left[ \frac{\text{Area}(\partial \mathcal{I})}{4G_N} + S_{\text{bulk}}(R \cup \mathcal{I}) \right] $$
Where:
* $\mathcal{I}$ represents the candidate island(s).
* $\partial \mathcal{I}$ is the Quantum Extremal Surface (QES).
* $S_{\text{bulk}}$ is the von Neumann entropy of matter fields in the union of the radiation region $R$ and the island $\mathcal{I}$.

**B. The Replica Trick and Gravitational Path Integral:**
To derive this rigorously, one computes the Renyi entropies $S_n$ using the replica trick:
$$ S_n(R) = \frac{1}{1-n} \ln \frac{Z(n)}{Z(1)^n} $$
In the presence of dynamical gravity, the gravitational path integral includes contributions from disconnected geometries (replica wormholes). The dominant saddle point shifts from a disconnected geometry (growing entropy) to a connected replica wormhole (decreasing entropy) when $t > t_{\text{Page}}$.

**C. The Page Curve Derivation:**
* **Phase I (Early Time):** The minimal surface is trivial ($\mathcal{I} = \emptyset$). $S_{\text{gen}} \approx S_{\text{bulk}}(R)$, which grows linearly with time.
* **Phase II (Late Time):** A new saddle appears where $\mathcal{I}$ is a region near the singularity/horizon. Due to the area term scaling with the shrinking black hole mass, the total $S_{\text{gen}}$ begins to decrease, matching the complement of the remaining black hole entropy.

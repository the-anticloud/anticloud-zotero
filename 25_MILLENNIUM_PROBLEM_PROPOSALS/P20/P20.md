#### 1) Core Hypothesis
The resolution to the Black Hole Information Paradox lies in the **Generalized Entropy Principle** applied to **Entanglement Islands**. 

Contrary to the semi-classical expectation that information is lost as the black hole evaporates (leading to mixed states), we hypothesize that the von Neumann entropy of the Hawking radiation, $S_{\text{rad}}$, does not grow indefinitely. Instead, at the "Page time," the quantum extremal surface (QES) defining the entanglement wedge of the radiation undergoes a topological phase transition. This transition incorporates a region inside the black hole horizon—the **Island**—into the entanglement wedge of the radiation. Consequently, the generalized entropy of the radiation decreases after the Page time, recovering the unitary Page curve and preserving information without violating local effective field theory outside the horizon.

Information is not stored on the stretched horizon but is non-locally encoded via the bulk-boundary correspondence, where the interior geometry emerges from the entanglement structure of the boundary state.
